# 04: Alex Glances at the Menu Bar

**Project:** my-torrent
**Created:** 2026-07-27
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Quickly understand what's happening with downloads/uploads, without opening the full main window.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Near-zero resource usage / unobtrusiveness
**Objective:** Success Criteria (idle resource usage) + Positioning differentiator ("lightweight, unobtrusive") — justifies the menu-bar-as-quick-access model from the Product Concept.

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Alex (Primary — sole ICP, per Product Brief)
**Situation:** Busy doing something else on his Mac; downloads are running in the background; wants a quick ambient check without breaking focus.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** One glance and everything's confirmed fine.

**Worry:** Will have to open an entire window just to check something trivial.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Desktop (Mac)
**Entry:** Looks at / hovers over / clicks the menu-bar icon while doing something else on the Mac.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Instantly sees aggregate download/upload speed (and possibly active download count), then returns to whatever he was doing.

**Business Success:**
Reinforces the "lightweight, unobtrusive" differentiator — justifies the menu-bar quick-access layer's reason to exist.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Меню-бар** — Alex hovers/clicks the menu-bar icon, sees aggregate download/upload speed at a glance ✓
