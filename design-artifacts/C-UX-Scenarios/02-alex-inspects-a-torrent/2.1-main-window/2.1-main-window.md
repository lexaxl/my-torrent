## Overview

**Page Purpose (this transaction):** Let Alex identify and select the torrent he wants to inspect, from the already-visible downloads list.

**Entry Context:** Alex is already in the main window (arrived via Scenario 01, or the app is simply open) and notices something worth checking on a specific torrent (slow speed, curiosity about included files).

**User Situation:** Mid-download, at his Mac, wants to check a specific torrent's status without disrupting the overall simple view.

**User Mental State:**
- **Hope:** Sees exactly what's happening without digging through menus.
- **Worry:** Will have to remember some obscure shortcut or menu item to find this.

**Discovery Method:** No separate arrival — starts from the list already being visible (this scenario begins mid-session, not at app launch).

**Main User Goal:** Quickly locate and select the torrent of interest.

**Business Goal:** Reinforces trust/transparency — supports the "power without bloat" positioning (Scenario 02's business goal).

**Exit Action (specific to this scenario):** Clicks a specific torrent's row → opens that torrent's detail view (step 2.2).

**Variants:** None.
